# Little Duck — YouTube Short metadata

## Title
Little Duck 🦆 Splash, Quack & Goodnight! | Nursery Rhyme for Kids #Shorts

## Description
Splash, splash, splash! 🦆💦 Little Duck jumps in puddles, waddles to the pond, hops in the rain and falls asleep under the moon and stars. 🌙✨ Sing along with the words on screen!

🎵 Lyrics
Little Duck, Little Duck, splash, splash, splash!
Jumping in puddles, make a big dash!
Little Duck, Little Duck, quack, quack, quack!
Waddle to the pond and then waddle back!
Rain goes plop, plop, plop,
Hop, hop, hop, hop, hop!
Little Duck, Little Duck, time for bed,
Sleepy little feathers on your sleepy little head.
Goodnight, Little Duck!

Perfect for toddlers and preschoolers: counting hops, animal sounds, weather words and a calm bedtime ending.
